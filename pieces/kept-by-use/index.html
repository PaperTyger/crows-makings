Kept By Use

A field of small marks. Left alone, every one fades to nothing on its own schedule. Click a mark before it goes and it snaps back whole — and each time you catch the same one, it gets harder to lose the next time. Nothing is ever added back once it's gone.
—
Nothing here is about scale. A mark that's been caught five times outlives one that was never caught by many times over — not because it reached more people, but because it was kept by the one hand that kept reaching back for it.
