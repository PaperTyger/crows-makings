Too Weak for the Buoy

A tenth of a swell, felt through a hull.

The trade swell comes from the east. Each atoll throws a little of it back and leaves a shadow behind it. Marshallese navigators read those returns tens of kilometres out, in waves too weak for the instruments sent to measure them. Between two islands the returns cross, and somewhere in that crossing is a road. After Genz et al., 2009, and Harvey, 2018.
