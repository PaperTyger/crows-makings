Where It Was Held

Someone held it by the foot and put it rim-first into the slip.

After a sixteenth-century Korean buncheong bowl in the Met. The white stops where the bucket's surface was. A level bucket and a still hand would leave a level line, and nobody's hand is still. The wrist tilts it, coming out ripples it, and a little runs. The line is the only record of the hand.
