For No Eye

A golden mole's fur shines green and violet. The golden mole is blind, and its eyes are covered by skin.
—
Each colour is computed from two-beam interference in a thin keratin layer, summed across the visible spectrum and converted to screen colour. The pelt's layout and the glint model are drawn, not measured. Mechanism after Snyder et al., Biology Letters, 2012.
