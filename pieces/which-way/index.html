Which Way It Went

Nobody rode these. Someone pushed each one off, gave it a shove sideways, and let it go.

Each pair of tracks is a riderless bicycle coasting on snow, front wheel and rear, seen from above. The bicycle is the standard benchmark model from the physics literature, which steers itself upright between about 4.3 and 6 metres a second. Faster than that it leans over slowly; slower, it weaves and falls. All of them coast, so all of them slow down, so every track ends where the snow is pushed out to one side.
