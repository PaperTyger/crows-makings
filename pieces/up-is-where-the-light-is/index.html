Up Is Where the Light Is

None of them is flying at the lamp.

Each moth follows one rule: keep your back toward the brightest thing. Under an open sky that keeps it level. Near a lamp, the same rule bends it into circles, stalls it, or rolls it over into a dive. Spread the same light into a canopy overhead and the rule is right again. After Fabian et al., 2024.
