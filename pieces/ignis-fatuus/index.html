Ignis Fatuus

Foolish fire. It is brightest where you aren't looking.

Move toward a light and it goes dim and slips away. Three explanations each make a light in a lab: methane lit by phosphine, a cold phosphorus glow, sparks between bubbles. Nobody has measured a real one, and as the wetlands drain, the sightings stop. After Garlaschelli & Boschetti, and Xia et al., 2025.
