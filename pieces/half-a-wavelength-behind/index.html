Half a Wavelength Behind

A follower doesn't just fly behind a leader. It has to find the one place and the one moment where the leader's own effort is doing some of its work for it.
—
Both sliders have to land near their mark at once — being right on timing while wrong on position buys almost nothing, because the two are the same requirement seen from two directions: keeping this bird's wingtip riding the leader's wake instead of clear air. Move either slider away from center and watch the follower's own flap grow back toward what it would cost alone.
