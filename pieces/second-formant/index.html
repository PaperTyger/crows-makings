The two faint horizontals mark 1000–3000 Hz, the window a whistle occupies. Everything outside it — the fundamental and its harmonic stack — is what whistling cannot carry at all.

Drag toward whistle and most of the picture leaves. What stays is a single sinuous line moving between four loci, the way whistled Spanish carries its vowels; consonants are in the transitions — the abrupt jumps and the interruptions — not in any band of their own.

The trade is the piece. Roughly 93% of the transmitted signal is discarded, and the trajectory survives it. That redundancy is what buys the range: a whistle carries ten times further than a shout.
