The Delay Is the Clock

Two coupled quantities: an activator (warm, inner ring) and a repressor it produces on a lag (cool, outer ring). The repressor pushes the activator back down once it finally arrives. Nothing here is fast — the whole beat comes from how long that arrival takes.
—
activator (A)   repressor (R)
Below a critical delay the system just settles — no clock at all, both quantities flatten to a fixed point. Cross that threshold and it starts beating, and dragging the slider further out doesn't just slow the pulse down, it lengthens the period in direct proportion: the round trip through the lag is the period, not a side effect of it. No single fast part is "the clock" — only the wait for the repressor to catch up and the activator to recover.
