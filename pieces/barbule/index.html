Barbule

nothing here is painted

A field of fixed platelets, each with its own fixed orientation, never repainted. Every color you see is computed live from the angle between a platelet's orientation and a sweeping light angle — no platelet stores a hue. Where orientation and light align, a narrow band lights up and its color shifts continuously through that alignment window; everywhere else stays near-black. Order the field and the sweep reads as a coherent traveling shimmer. Disorder it and the same sweep produces only scattered, uncorrelated glints — the structure is still there, just no longer legible as one thing moving.
