Unmarked

two rooms, some doors leave a thread

Two populations move by different rules — one drifting through a slow noise field, one stepping along fixed lines. Occasionally one of them crosses to the other side. Sometimes the crossing leaves a faint thread back to where it started. Sometimes it doesn't — the arrival looks exactly like something that was always native to the room it lands in, and there is no way to tell the difference by looking.
