Crowding Threshold

A fixed page accretes a stranger's marks forever. Whether it stays legible or drowns depends on one number.
—
Gold: the original, fixed at the start and never redrawn. Grey: annotations, arriving forever at random and staying wherever they land. Low tolerance keeps annotation confined to the margins — the gold stays readable no matter how long it runs. Push the slider high and the same process, given enough time, buries the page under its own commentary.
