Echo-Ranging

An orb-weaver's crouch: a probe pulse sent out through its own web, read back by the frequency of what returns.
—
Every few seconds the spider crouches — a pulse leaves the hub on every spoke at once. Only the spoke actually carrying prey sends anything back. Closer prey rings a higher note, but quieter; farther prey rings lower, but louder. Move the slider and watch both change together, in opposite directions.
