Consensus Drift

the wash always wins, eventually

A field of cells, each slowly chasing a drifting target it never gets to question — repetition eroding toward its own average until belief and reinforcement are the same thing. Rarely, a cell is struck by something the field cannot absorb: it cuts clean against the wash, then dilutes back in, on a slower clock than everything around it.
