Edge-on

One veil, one thickness, one colour of glass. Nothing here is painted blue.

Ozone is a pale blue gas, too faint to tint the noon sky, because noon light crosses its layer straight down. At dusk the light runs along the layer instead. The veil's absorption here is shaped like ozone's Chappuis band: red and green go, blue mostly stays. The "veil in the frame" number barely moves as you lean. The amount of veil is the same; it only gets crowded.
